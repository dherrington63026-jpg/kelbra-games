Crude Outlaws Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Crude Outlaws is a PC game for Steam made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account of ours is needed. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. Steam handles your login.
  • Your saves and settings stay on your own PC.
  • The game sends nothing to us automatically. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • A problem report or crash report is sent only if you choose to send one.
  • Co-op runs through Steam. Other players in your game see your Steam name. There is no voice chat and the game never uses your microphone.
  • You buy the game once, on Steam. We never see your payment details.
  • The game has Western violence and, in the full game, mature themes. It is meant for older teens and adults.

What stays on your PC

Your world, your progress and your settings are saved on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CrudeOutlaws\Saved\SaveGames. The free demo keeps its own separate saves. We do not receive them. Steam Cloud saves are planned for later; this page will be updated before they are added.

Problem reports and crash reports (only if you send one)

The pause menu has a "Report a bug" button. After a crash, the game may also offer to send a crash report. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. A report can contain:

  • the note you type;
  • the game version;
  • where you were in the game world and how long you had played;
  • your PC's graphics card and memory size;
  • the end of the game's log file, which may include your Steam name as it appears in the game's log;
  • the crash's own files (for a crash report);
  • your world save, but only if you tick the box to include it.

A report goes to our private inbox. It is a private spreadsheet that only we can open, filled in through Google's Apps Script service on our behalf. A copy also stays on your PC in the game's Saved\BugReports folder. We use reports only to find and fix problems.

Steam

Crude Outlaws is sold and run through Steam, and uses Steam for friend invites, for joining co-op games and, when they are added, achievements and cloud saves. Steam collects its own information under Valve's privacy policy: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

Playing with other people

Co-op works over Steam only. We do not run any servers. The other players in your game can see your Steam name and what you do in the game world. The game has no voice chat and never uses your microphone.

Made with the help of AI tools

Some of the game's 3D models, textures and pictures (such as posters, paintings and signs) were made with AI tools and then finished and checked by hand. The characters' spoken voices were made with AI text-to-speech. Everything else, including the game itself, its code, world, quests and writing, is made by us. Music: "Wild West Music" by Harvey Carman (harveycarman.com), CC BY 4.0. Other licensed content is listed in the game's credits.

Deleting your data

  • Saves and settings: delete the CrudeOutlaws\Saved folder shown above, or uninstall the game.
  • A problem report you sent: email us and we will delete it. We do not keep an account or profile about you, so there is usually nothing else to delete.
  • Anything Steam holds: use your Steam account settings and Valve's privacy policy.

Children

Crude Outlaws is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
