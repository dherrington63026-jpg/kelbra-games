Hush Money Motel Help & Support

A co-op horror game about running a motel with dark secrets, for 1 to 4 players on Steam. Made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC, Missouri.

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net
Please tell us what happened, what you were doing, and your PC (Windows version, graphics card).

How do I play with friends?

Hush Money Motel is co-op for up to 4 players over Steam. Start the game, then invite your friends through the Steam friends list, or join a friend who has invited you. Everyone needs the same version of the game.

Where are my saves and settings?

Saves are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HushMoneyMotel\Saved\SaveGames on your PC. Settings and key choices stay on each PC and are not shared between players.

How do I report a bug or a crash?

Open the Esc menu and choose Report a Bug, then type what happened. If the game crashed, the next time you start it the main menu asks whether to send the crash report; you choose Send it or No thanks. You can also just email us.

Does it have voice chat?

No. The game has no voice chat and never uses your microphone. Most players talk with friends on Discord or Steam voice while they play.

Does it work with a controller or on Steam Deck?

A controller is supported (move, look, use, flashlight, desk choices and menus, and the on-screen hints switch to controller buttons), but it has not been tested with every controller. Steam Deck has not been tested yet.

What kind of content is in the game?

Horror and dark crime themes: murder, kidnapping and drug dealing in the story, blood to clean up (there is a Less blood setting, see below), a body in a bathtub, frightening supernatural characters and sudden jump scares. Gunshots and violence happen off screen. Drugs are hidden and talked about, never shown being used.

Can I play with less blood?

Yes. Open Settings and set Blood to Less. Rooms then show a dull dried stain instead of blood pools and wall spatter, the trunk has no blood, and the job reads “scrub the stain”. It is each player's own choice: in co-op, everyone sees their own setting. The story and the horror themes stay the same.

I cannot join my friend (co-op problems)

Both players need Steam running and the same version of the game. Let Steam finish any update first, then restart the game.

Invite your friend through the Steam friends list.

If it still fails, try turning off a VPN, and check that your firewall allows the game and Steam.

Email us what happened on both PCs and we will help.

The game will not start or it crashes

Update your graphics card drivers, then restart your PC.

In Steam, right-click the game, choose Properties, then Installed Files, then "Verify integrity of game files".

Check that your PC meets the requirements listed on the game's Steam page.

If it still crashes, email us the details. If the game offers to send a crash report, saying yes helps us a lot.

The game runs slowly

Open Settings and lower the graphics level (the Low and Medium levels run on much older PCs), or turn on the frame limit or V-sync.

Close other programs, especially browsers and video apps, while you play.

Tell us your graphics card and what happened and we will look into it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Steam. Steam lets you request a refund for any reason within 14 days of buying a game if you have played it for less than 2 hours. Start here: Steam refunds.
