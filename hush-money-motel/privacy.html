Hush Money Motel Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Hush Money Motel is a PC game for Steam made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account of ours is needed. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The game uses only your Steam sign-in.
  • Your saves and settings stay on your own PC.
  • The game sends nothing to us automatically. There are no ads, no purchases inside the game, no analytics and no tracking.
  • A bug report or crash report is sent only if you press Send (or answer yes after a crash).
  • Co-op runs through Steam. We run no servers. There is no voice chat and the game never uses your microphone.
  • You buy the game once, on Steam. We never see your payment details.
  • The game is horror with dark crime themes and is meant for older teens and adults.

What stays on your PC

Your progress and saves are kept on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HushMoneyMotel\Saved\SaveGames (files ending in .sav). Your settings and key choices also stay on your PC. We do not receive any of this. Steam Cloud saves are planned for later; this page will be updated before they are added.

Bug reports and crash reports (only if you send one)

You can send a report from the Esc menu (Report a Bug). After a crash, the next time you start the game the main menu asks "Send the crash report to the developer?" with the choices Send it or No thanks. Nothing is sent unless you press Send or answer yes. A report can contain:

  • the note you type, the game version and the time;
  • your PC details: Windows version, processor, graphics card, memory, graphics setting and screen size;
  • where you were in the game: your position, the room, the day and time, your money, your loan progress, the difficulty, your story progress, and whether you were playing solo, hosting or joined;
  • the end of the game's log file, which can include your computer's name and your Windows user name;
  • your save file, but only if you tick the box to include it;
  • for a crash report: the crash's own folder, with its log, the engine's crash notes and a crash dump, which is a snapshot of the game's memory at the moment it crashed.

A report is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a private Google Apps Script web page on our Google account, which stores it where only we can read it. A copy also stays on your PC in the game's Saved\BugReports folder. We use reports only to find and fix problems.

Steam

Hush Money Motel is sold and run through Steam, and uses Steam for friend invites, for joining co-op games and for achievements. Steam collects its own information under Valve's privacy policy: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

Playing with other people

Co-op works over Steam's own networking, through Steam's relay servers. We do not run any servers. You join through Steam invites and your Steam friends list. The game does not show player names on screen, but Steam's overlay shows Steam names. The game has no voice chat and never uses your microphone; players who want to talk use Discord or Steam voice outside the game.

Made with the help of AI tools

Some of this game's content was made with AI tools and then reviewed and edited by us: the spoken voices (made with the text-to-speech tools Kokoro and Chatterbox), the radio songs (made with ACE-Step, with lyrics written by the developer), pictures and textures (made with Z-Image-Turbo), and some 3D props and bodies (made with TRELLIS). Most of the people in the game come from Epic's City Sample crowd and MetaHuman. Everything else, including the game itself, its code, world, story and writing, is made by us. Other licensed content is listed in the game's Credits page.

Deleting your data

  • Saves and settings: delete the HushMoneyMotel\Saved folder shown above, or uninstall the game.
  • A report you sent: email us and we will delete it. We do not keep an account or profile about you, so there is usually nothing else to delete.
  • Anything Steam holds: use your Steam account settings and Valve's privacy policy.

Children

Hush Money Motel is horror with dark crime themes. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
