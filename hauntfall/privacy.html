Hauntfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Hauntfall is a horror survival game published by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”), 117 S Lexington St Ste 100, Harrisonville, MO 64701, Missouri, USA. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics, no purchases and no accounts, and it keeps your progress on your own device.

What stays on your device

Hauntfall does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or your phone’s advertising ID.

The game saves your progress (your hero, gear, gems, the church you build, survivors, quests, achievements and your settings) on your own device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, deletes it. We never receive a copy.

No ads, no tracking

Hauntfall has no ads, and we do not plan to add any. It has no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect information about how you play. The game does not connect to any server of ours while you play, and it includes no third-party software that collects data.

No purchases

The game is free, and this version has nothing to buy. We do not see or store any payment details.

If you play in a web browser

Hauntfall can also be played in a web browser. Like any website, the company that hosts the page can see your device’s IP address when the page is loaded. Your progress is stored in your browser on your own device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

About the art

The pictures in the game (monsters, characters, places, items and icons) were created with AI image tools, and the music was synthesized by us. None of it shows real people or real places.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. If you would like to ask us anything about your information, email support@kelbrasolutions.net. We will answer within 30 days.

Age

Hauntfall contains scary, dark fantasy horror themes and cartoon-style combat violence against monsters. It is intended for players aged 13 and over (see the age rating shown in the app store). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@kelbrasolutions.net
