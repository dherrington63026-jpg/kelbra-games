Hauntfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (DRAFT: ads version, publish only when the game with ads ships)

Hauntfall is a horror survival game published by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”), 117 S Lexington St Ste 100, Harrisonville, MO 64701, Missouri, USA. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device, has no accounts and no analytics of our own, shows ads between runs and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, and offers two optional one-time purchases (Remove Ads and a Builders Pack).

What stays on your device

Hauntfall does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. The game itself does not read your phone’s advertising ID; the ad service described below may (see “Optional rewarded ads”).

The game saves your progress (your hero, gear, gems, the church you build, survivors, quests, achievements and your settings) on your own device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, deletes it. We never receive a copy.

Ads

Hauntfall shows a short ad after every second finished run (when you tap to continue), and offers optional rewarded ads: for example, “Watch an ad” to come back once after a defeat, to double the gems you earned in a run, or to unlock an extra builder. Ads are never shown while you are playing a run, and never in AFK mode. Rewarded ads are always your choice. Everything a rewarded ad gives can also be earned by playing. The one-time Remove Ads purchase stops the ads between runs and gives you the rewards for free.

The ad network is Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC). To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location), device and operating-system information, and how you interact with ads. See Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data from Google and we do not sell data.

  • iPhone: if the game asks to track you, you can choose “Ask App Not to Track”. Ads still work; they are then non-personalized. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Where required, the game shows a consent choice before ads and respects it. The ad network is set for a teen audience, and we block adult, gambling and misleading ad categories.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the game stops offering ads.
  • The game has no analytics of our own. The only third-party software in the game that collects data is the ad service above and the purchase service described under Purchases.

Optional bug reports

The pause menu has a “Report a problem” button. A report is only sent when you tap Send. It contains the note you write, your device model and system version, the app version, any error text, and (if you leave the box ticked) your game progress. We use it only to find and fix bugs, we never sell it, and it holds no name or email unless you type one into your note. If the game crashed, it may offer to send the error report on the next start, and again only if you tap Send.

Purchases

The game is free. It offers two optional, one-time purchases: Remove Ads (stops the ads between runs and gives you every ad reward for free) and a Builders Pack (unlocks two extra builders in the church right away; they can also be earned by playing). Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store); we never see your card or payment details. To confirm that you own the purchase and to restore it on a new device, the game uses the purchase service RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipts from the store. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Nothing you buy gives a gameplay advantage that cannot also be earned by playing.

If you play in a web browser

Hauntfall can also be played in a web browser (the web version has no ads). Like any website, the company that hosts the page can see your device’s IP address when the page is loaded. Your progress is stored in your browser on your own device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

About the art

The pictures in the game (monsters, characters, places, items and icons) were created with AI image tools, and the music was synthesized by us. None of it shows real people or real places.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. If you would like to ask us anything about your information, email support@kelbrasolutions.net. We will answer within 30 days.

Age

Hauntfall contains scary, dark fantasy horror themes and cartoon-style combat violence against monsters. It is intended for players aged 13 and over (see the age rating shown in the app store). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
