Hauntfall Support

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net and we will reply as soon as we can. It helps to tell us your phone model and what happened.

How do I play?

Put your finger anywhere on the screen and drag to move your hero. Your weapon fires on its own at the nearest monster. Every few kills you level up and pick one of three upgrades. Survive until the boss appears, beat it, and the next district opens.

What are Dash and Burst?

Tap Dash to dash a short distance in the direction you are moving. You cannot be hurt for a moment, so use it to slip through a crowd. Burst fills as you kill: when it glows, tap it to blast everything around you.

What is an Evolution?

If you hold the right pair of upgrades, a golden card appears at level-up that fuses them into something stronger, for example Salt Circle and Molotov become Hellfire Ring.

The church

Between runs you return to your haunted church. Tap a + to build a room. Rooms make power, water, food, scrap and gold, or boost your hero. When someone knocks at the front door, let them in: survivors staff rooms and make them better. Rooms take real time to build, so come back later. At night the dead may attack the church; you can defend it yourself or let your defenses hold the line.

Gear, gems and the Forge

Monsters drop gear in six rarities. Open Gear to equip it, upgrade it, add gems and combine spare items at the Forge. Weapons can carry perks. Spend Echoes (earned in every run) on permanent Talents.

Rank, heroes and companions

Every run earns Rank XP, and every rank pays a reward. New heroes and companion pets unlock as your rank grows: change them with the Hero button on the church screen.

Is the game too fast?

Open the pause menu (the II button) and pick Slow, Normal or Fast under Game speed. You can also turn Music and Sounds off there.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device. If you delete the app or clear its data, progress is removed. We do not use accounts, so we cannot restore it for you.

Why are there no ads?

Hauntfall is free, and it has no ads and nothing to buy in this version.

Is it scary?

It is a dark horror game: ghosts, zombies and other monsters, a gloomy town, and fantasy combat violence against monsters. There is no gore aimed at people and no real-world violence. It is meant for players 13 and over.

Is the art made by AI?

Yes. The pictures were created with AI image tools, and the music was synthesized by us, so nothing shows real people or places.
