Alikeberry Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Alikeberry is a puzzle game made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account is needed. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your own device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google collects some device and usage information, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google and checked through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • Alikeberry is for players aged 13 and over.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, streak, settings (such as text size and sound) and a copy of what you have bought are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of Alikeberry shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads never appear in the middle of a puzzle. You can choose to watch a short ad to get extra hints ("Watch an ad"), and a short ad may appear between puzzles from time to time.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking);
  • your approximate location, worked out from your internet (IP) address;
  • information about the ads you see and tap;
  • basic device information and crash or performance data.

Google uses this information as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you still see ads, but they are not personalized to you. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings, Privacy, Ads (the exact wording depends on your phone).
  • Consent message: in some places (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states) the app shows a consent message from Google before ads are personalized. You can make your choice there.
  • No ads at all: a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in the app removes every ad, including the optional "Watch an ad" offers.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Purchases such as Remove Ads, All Modes or theme packs are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To unlock what you bought, the app checks your purchases through RevenueCat, which receives the store receipt and a random app identifier. It does not receive your name or email address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. Use "Restore purchases" in the app menu if you change devices.

Deleting your data

  • Progress and settings: open the menu, then Settings, then "Erase my progress", or delete the app. Both remove everything the app stored on your device.
  • Purchases: these stay with your Apple ID or Google account and come back with "Restore purchases".
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone settings, as described under "Your ad choices".
  • Anything else: email support@kelbrasolutions.net and we will help. We do not hold an account or profile about you, so there is usually nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Alikeberry is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that shared personal information, email us and we will help.

Pictures

The pictures in some puzzle modes were created with artificial intelligence image software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
