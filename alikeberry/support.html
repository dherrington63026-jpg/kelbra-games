Alikeberry Help & Support

Alikeberry is a calm sorting puzzle game by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC, Missouri.

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net
Please tell us your phone model and what happened.

How do I play?

Every level lists the groups to find at the top (for example "Fruits" or "1980s"). Tap or drag a card from the bottom of a column into a box at the top. An empty box takes any card; after that, only cards from the same group fit, and the box shows its group's name. Fill every box to win. Some cards fit two groups, so think it through. Undo is always free.

In the "Four Groups" play style, every card is on the table: tap the cards that belong together, then press Submit. You can switch play styles in the menu.

I ran out of hints

Every level gives you 3 new hints. If you run out in the middle of a level, you can choose to watch a short ad for 3 more, or just keep playing. If you bought Remove Ads, extra hints are free.

When do ads show?

Never in the middle of a puzzle. A short ad may show between puzzles, at most once every 4 puzzles. You never see one during your first 10 minutes after opening the game, or at all the first time you open it each day. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase removes all ads for good.

I paid, but my purchase is not unlocked

Open the menu and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure your phone is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. If it still does not work, email us with the date of the purchase.

I got a new phone

Your progress is saved on the phone you play on, so it does not move to a new phone. Your purchases do: on the new phone, open the menu and tap "Restore purchases".

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them. iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & history.

How do I delete my data?

Everything the game saves is on your phone. Open the menu, then Settings, then "Erase my progress", or delete the app. To reset the advertising ID used for ads, see your phone's privacy settings. More details are in the privacy policy.

The text is too small

Open the menu, then Settings, then "Text size", and choose Large or Extra large.
