Sandbag Squad Help & Support

A cartoon co-op flood-night game for 1 to 4 players on Steam. Made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC, Missouri.

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net
Please tell us what happened, what you were doing, and your PC (Windows version, graphics card).

How do I play with friends?

Sandbag Squad is co-op for up to 4 players. One player hosts the game on their PC and shares the join code (it looks like SBSQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) with friends they trust; the friends enter the code to join. Everyone needs the same version of the game. Steam invites will be added later.

Where are my saves and photos?

In the game's Saved folder in your user AppData folder (normally %LOCALAPPDATA%\SandbagSquad\Saved). Photo-mode pictures (P key) are in the Photos folder there.

How do I report a bug?

The game has no report button. Please email us what happened, which night you were on and whether you were playing alone or with friends. A crash writes a log file in the Saved folder; if you can attach it, that helps.

Does it have voice chat?

No. In the game you can only point out things to friends with the ping arrow (T key). Most players talk on Discord or Steam voice while they play.

Does it work with a controller or on Steam Deck?

An Xbox-style gamepad is supported with full controls (it has not been tried on every controller yet). Steam Deck has not been tested yet.

What kind of content is in the game?

Cartoon flooding with slapstick fun: players can shove friends into the water and throw mud. No blood, no gore, no injuries or death shown, and no bad language.

I cannot join my friend (co-op problems)

Both players need the same version of the game. Make sure you typed the whole join code (it looks like SBSQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) and that the host is still in the game.

If it still fails, try turning off a VPN, and check that your firewall allows the game.

Email us what happened on both PCs and we will help.

The game will not start or it crashes

Update your graphics card drivers, then restart your PC.

In Steam, right-click the game, choose Properties, then Installed Files, then "Verify integrity of game files".

Check that your PC meets the requirements listed on the game's Steam page.

If it still crashes, email us the details. A crash writes a log file in the game's Saved folder; if you can attach it to your email, that helps us a lot.

The game runs slowly

Open Settings and lower the graphics level (the Low and Medium levels run on much older PCs), or turn on the frame limit or V-sync.

Close other programs, especially browsers and video apps, while you play.

Tell us your graphics card and what happened and we will look into it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Steam. Steam lets you request a refund for any reason within 14 days of buying a game if you have played it for less than 2 hours. Start here: Steam refunds.
