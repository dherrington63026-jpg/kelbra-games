Sandbag Squad Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Sandbag Squad is a PC game for Steam made by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account is needed, and the game never asks for your name, email address, phone number or anything else about you.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your own PC.
  • The game sends nothing to us. There are no ads, no purchases inside the game, no analytics and no tracking, and no crash reports are sent.
  • Co-op is peer to peer: one player hosts on their own PC and friends join with a join code. We run no servers.
  • There is no voice chat and the game never uses your microphone.
  • You buy the game once, on Steam. We never see your payment details.

What stays on your PC

The game saves a few small files in its Saved folder, in your user AppData folder on your PC (normally %LOCALAPPDATA%\SandbagSquad\Saved):

  • SSProgress.txt: your money, upgrades, night, stars, hats, gnomes and endless-mode record;
  • SSVolume.txt: your sound settings;
  • SSLastJoin.txt: the last join code you used;
  • Photos: the pictures you take in photo mode (P key).

We do not receive any of these. Steam Cloud saves may be added later; this page will be updated before that happens. If the game crashes it only writes a log file on your own PC; nothing is sent to us.

Playing with other people (join codes)

Co-op is peer to peer: one player hosts the game on their own PC, and there are no servers of ours. Friends join with a join code that looks like SBSQ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. That code is the host's internet address (IP address and port) in coded form, so the host should only share it with people they trust. The game copies the code for you; it never sends it anywhere. Later, Steam invites will also be added, and those go through Steam's own networking.

Other players in your game see the worker character names in the game (Mia, Sam, Jo and Ray). They do not see your Steam name or your real name.

Steam

Sandbag Squad is sold and run through Steam. Steam collects its own information under Valve's privacy policy: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement.

Made with the help of AI tools

Some game content was made with AI tools run on our own computer: the textures, the 3D models (houses, trees, the truck, the pump, the sandbags and household items) and the four worker characters (pictures made with Z-Image-Turbo and turned into 3D with Microsoft TRELLIS), and the mud splat pictures. All AI content was reviewed and edited by us. The music and sound effects were made by us from scratch with our own code (no AI). Character animations are Epic Games' free Mannequin animations, and the characters were rigged with Reallusion's free AccuRIG tool. No AI is used while you play.

Deleting your data

  • Saves and settings: delete the SandbagSquad\Saved folder shown above, or uninstall the game.
  • Anything Steam holds: use your Steam account settings and Valve's privacy policy.
  • We do not collect or keep any information about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Sandbag Squad does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
