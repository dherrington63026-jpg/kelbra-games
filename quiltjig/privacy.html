Quiltjig Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Quiltjig is a jigsaw puzzle game published by Kelbra Business Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”), 117 S Lexington St Ste 100, Harrisonville, MO 64701, Missouri, USA. This policy explains what information the game uses. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it keeps your puzzles on your own device.

What stays on your device

Quiltjig does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or your phone’s advertising ID.

The game saves your progress (puzzles in progress, finished pictures, your daily streak, the items you own and your settings) on your own device. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

No ads, no tracking

Quiltjig has no ads, and it never will. It has no analytics and no tracking, and it does not show you personalized anything. We do not collect information about how you play.

Purchases

The game is free to play. The app offers optional purchases (picture packs, table backgrounds, piece styles, sounds and music) and an optional subscription called Quiltjig Club. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details.

To unlock what you bought, the app uses a purchase service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipts from Apple or Google and a random anonymous ID created for your installation. That ID is not your name or email address and does not identify you. Like any internet service, RevenueCat’s servers also see your device’s IP address when the app contacts them. RevenueCat uses this only so the app can tell which items you own and can restore them on a new phone. It does not use it to track you across other apps or websites. We do not sell your information. RevenueCat’s own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

About the pictures

Every picture in the game was created with AI image tools. They are not photographs of real places or people. Picture, music and sound credits are listed inside the game under Credits.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. To ask about purchase records held by the purchase service, or to ask us to delete anything we may hold about you, email support@kelbrasolutions.net and tell us your phone type and roughly when you bought something. We will answer within 30 days.

Age

Quiltjig is a general-audience puzzle game intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@kelbrasolutions.net
