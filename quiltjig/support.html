Quiltjig Support

Need help? Email support@kelbrasolutions.net and we will reply as soon as we can. It helps to tell us your phone model and what happened.

How do I play?

Pick a picture and a number of pieces. Pieces wait in the tray at the bottom. Drag one up onto the board and let go near where it belongs: it clicks into place. Pieces that fit each other also click together, so you can build little islands anywhere on the table.

How do I zoom?

Pinch with two fingers, or tap the + and − buttons. Drag an empty spot with one finger to move the table. Tap Fit to see the whole picture again.

Helpers

Edges shows only the border pieces. Hold Peek to see the finished picture. Faint picture shows a ghost of the picture on the board. All helpers are free, with no limits.

What is the Daily Puzzle?

A new picture every day, the same for everyone. Finishing it on the day keeps your streak going. If you miss a day the streak starts again and your best streak is kept.

Where is my progress saved?

On your own device. If you delete the app or clear its data, progress is removed. We do not use accounts.

Restoring purchases

If you bought something and it is missing (for example after getting a new phone), open the Shop tab and tap Restore purchases at the bottom, or open Settings. Purchases belong to your Apple ID or Google account, so sign in to the same one.

Refunds and subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. To ask for a refund, or to cancel or change the Quiltjig Club subscription, use your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Why are there no ads?

Because nobody likes them. Quiltjig is free, and it is supported by people who choose optional extras such as picture packs, table backgrounds and music.

Are the pictures real photos?

No. Every picture was created with AI image tools, so a “place” may not exist.
